SafeAlert is being developed to bring emergency contacts, location-aware information, weather, and community safety updates together in a friendly mobile experience.

Features and availability can change while the app is in development. Some flows may be demonstrations and may not send alerts or contact emergency services. Do not rely on SafeAlert in place of 911 or other official emergency services.
