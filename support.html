SafeAlert help

We’re getting things ready.

SafeAlert is in development. This support page is a preview while the official support channel is being set up.

Need emergency help now?

Call 911 for immediate danger or urgent medical help. SafeAlert does not dispatch responders and should not be used as a substitute for 911 or official emergency alerts.

Before using the app

  • Check that location and notification permissions match your preferences.
  • Keep emergency contacts and local emergency numbers available outside the app.
  • Some app features are prototypes and may not send messages or alerts.

Official support

A verified support email and release-specific help instructions have not yet been provided. This draft intentionally does not direct users to send personal, medical, or precise-location information through a public channel.
